# AcadFlow Ethics & Decision Support Principles

1. **Decision Support, Never Autonomous Judgment**:
   Risk radar scores and attendance buffers are strictly indicators to alert students, mentors, and faculty for timely intervention. They must never be used to autonomously fail a student or deny examination access.

2. **Explainability & Transparency**:
   Every risk calculation or buffer metric must expose its input factors (classes missed, assignment lateness, historical submission rate).

3. **Privacy & Data Minimization**:
   Role-based access control guarantees that students cannot inspect peer data, and audit logging captures all coordinator and mentor profile views.
